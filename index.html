Why Godavari food tastes like this

The delta gives us rice, coconut and river fish. The summers give us raw mangoes and red chillies. Out of these come the things Godavari homes are known for: fiery avakaya, prawn and chicken pickles, laddus rolled by hand, and chekkalu fried fresh for the evening tea.

That is the food we grew up with, and it is what we make at TNS.
